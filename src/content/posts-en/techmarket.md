**TechMarket** is an online store for tech products (electronics, gaming, and accessories).

- **Repository:** [github.com/Judarin15/TechMarket](https://github.com/Judarin15/TechMarket)
- **Tech stack:** React, Vite, and Supabase. I designed the interface in Figma first.

## Features

- **Storefront:** catalog with filters by price, brand, and category, search, promotional banners, favorites, cart, and checkout with stock validation.
- **Customer account:** sign-up and login, profile with shipping details, and order history with a "buy again" option.
- **Admin panel:** products with prices and discounts, categories, orders and their statuses, coupons, carousel banners, admin users, and customers.
- **Responsive** design across every view.

## From prototype to a real backend

The first version used MockAPI as a test backend. I then migrated it to **Supabase**, which provides a PostgreSQL database, authentication, and a ready-to-use API.
