**[Galaxia Sonora](https://galaxiasonora.com/)** is my Spanish-language audiobook project.

I built an online store that works as a single sales hub: it brings together the audiobooks published on **Hotmart** and **Etsy**, so customers can find them in one place and buy on the platform they prefer.

- **Website:** [galaxiasonora.com](https://galaxiasonora.com/)
